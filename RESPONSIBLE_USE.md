# Responsible use

Penteos is built for **authorized** security testing: penetration tests, red-team style
assessments and research on systems you own or have explicit, written permission to test.

- **Get permission first.** Testing systems without authorization is illegal in most countries,
  even when no damage is done. Keep the written authorization (scope, dates, contacts) of every
  engagement.
- **Stay inside the scope.** Penteos enforces the scope you define - every tool, request and AI
  action is checked against it - but the scope is only as good as what you enter. Review it.
- **You are responsible for what Penteos does on your behalf.** AI agents act within your scope,
  your risk ceiling and your approvals; their actions are yours. Review what they propose.
- **Protect your clients' data.** Engagement data stays on your machine (or your team server).
  When you use a cloud AI provider, review Penteos's privacy settings before sending client data
  to it.
